How this was built / assumptions
  • Year: 2026 — every dated item matches the weekday implied by its class (Tue quizzes, Wed homework, Mon/Wed 9:30 AM DRAMA classes).
  • Date ranges (Evaluative Quiz 2 & 3, both Final Exams) are placed on the earliest date; the full range is shown in the details.
  • Weekly items run from the first full week of class to the last week before finals: ACCT 467 quizzes Sep 15–Dec 8, ACCT 432 homework Sep 16–Dec 2, DRAMA 247 readings Sep 14–Dec 2.
  • DRAMA 247 readings are skipped on any date that already has another DRAMA 247 assignment, per the "EXCEPT on days with other assignments" note.
  • Weighting: items with no stated percentage are treated as <5%. 6–19% is bold, 20%+ is extra bold.
  • Reading Week (Nov 9–13) is excluded — no weekly quizzes, homework or readings are generated in that week.
  • Other statutory holidays are not excluded from the weekly items, since the source file doesn't list them. Uncheck anything that doesn't apply.
